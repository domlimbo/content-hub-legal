Privacy Policy

Content Hub · Effective November 20, 2026

Content Hub is a personal, single-user desktop tool. Its owner uses it to schedule and publish short videos to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts and to view analytics for those accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When an account owner connects a TikTok or YouTube account, the app requests only these permissions:

  • Basic profile (display name, account ID): to show which account is connected.
  • Account statistics (follower, like and video counts): for the owner's analytics dashboard.
  • Video list and video statistics (views, likes, comments, shares): for the owner's analytics dashboard.
  • Video publishing: to upload videos the owner chose, at times the owner chose.

Where data is stored

  • All data is stored locally on the owner's own computer. There is no Content Hub server or cloud database.
  • OAuth access credentials are stored in the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager) and are never written to files or logs.
  • Video files stay in the owner's local folders and are sent only to the platform the owner chose to post to.

Sharing

Content Hub does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is only sent to TikTok and YouTube through their official APIs to perform the actions described above. If the owner turns on optional caption drafting, the video's working title and the owner's style notes are sent to the Anthropic API to draft a caption. No TikTok or YouTube account data is included in that request.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting an account in Content Hub deletes its stored credentials immediately.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps and services permissions) or in the Google Account permissions page.
  • Removing the app's local data folder deletes all stored analytics and history.

Children

Content Hub is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
